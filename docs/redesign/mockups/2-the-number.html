Cost-burden adjustment (gross)
+764,476 units gross · net 504,627 after the overlap deduction · 37% of the total

What it is

Households paying more than 30% of income on housing. Region 43.19% vs. nation 31.45% = 11.74 points, applied to about 6.5 million occupied households.

What the statute says

65584.01(b)(1)(H): HCD considers the share of households that are cost burdened in the region and nationally.
65584.01(b)(3): HCD “may accept or reject the information provided by the council of governments or modify its own assumptions or methodology.”

What is HCD’s judgment call

Everything about how the gap becomes a unit count. The statute names the factor but has no formula. HCD applies the gap to all households, including moderate and above-moderate.
